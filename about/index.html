A little more about me.

Engineering is the career I’m passionate about pursuing, but there’s plenty more to me outside of it. I am Ukrainian, which has heavily defined my identity and upbringing. I speak both Russian and English, and in my free time I am trying to learn French and German. My friends and family mean the world to me and I always enjoy going out and interacting with different people from different backgrounds. Here are a few more of the interests, hobbies, and experiences that make up the rest of my life.
